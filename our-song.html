Questions

Our Song, answered

How long does it take?

Every song is delivered within 2–3 weeks of receiving your completed questionnaire and payment. Need it sooner for an upcoming date? Let us know and we'll do our best to accommodate.

How will we receive the song?

Your finished, fully produced track is sent straight to the email address you provide in the questionnaire, ready to download and play.

Can we request changes?

We want you to love it. Get in touch after listening and we'll talk through any tweaks to lyrics or style before it's finalised.

What if we don't know what to write?

That's completely normal! Answer what you can, even in a few words — we'll ask a couple of follow-up questions by email if we need more detail.
